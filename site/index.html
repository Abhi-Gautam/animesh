macOS

macOS 13 or newer

Apple Silicon .dmgIntel .dmg

Move Animesh into Applications and open it. Choose Open Animesh from the menu bar to open the window.

The current download is not notarized. macOS may require approval in Privacy & Security. Homebrew builds from source and is another install option.

Install with Homebrew
brew tap abhi-gautam/animesh https://github.com/Abhi-Gautam/animesh
brew install abhi-gautam/animesh/animesh
animesh service start

Homebrew builds the full app locally, so the first install takes longer.

Before you install

Does Animesh play or download episodes?

It tracks release schedules and sends reminders. Watch episodes through your streaming service. Source release times do not guarantee availability on a particular service or in your region.

Does it work offline?

Your saved library and known schedules remain available offline. Searching, discovering titles, and updating release times need an internet connection to AniList and TVmaze.

What leaves my device?

Searches and schedule refreshes contact AniList and TVmaze. Your library is stored locally; Animesh has no account, cloud sync, or app analytics. Those sources have their own privacy policies.

Why didn’t I get a reminder?

Check Health in the app for notification permission, source freshness, and the background service. Your system’s Focus or Do Not Disturb settings can silence banners. A show without a known upcoming episode has no reminder scheduled.

How do I report a problem?

Open a GitHub issue with your system, app version, and steps to reproduce it. Health can copy diagnostics; review them before sharing.
